# Privacy Policy

Inversiones Felix is a personal tool used only by its owner, Felix Berasategui, on his own Google account.

What the app accesses: read-only access to the owner's own Gmail messages carrying a specific label (daily broker account statements) and to the owner's own Google Sheet.

What it does with the data: it extracts figures from those statements and writes them to the owner's own Google Sheet.

Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. No data from any other user is collected, because there are no other users.

Storage: data stays in the owner's own Google account. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
